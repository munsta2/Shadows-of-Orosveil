
## **Session Recap: Embers of the Past**

### **Arrival at Emberhold**

The party reached the towering mountain fortress of **Emberhold**, seat of **House Emberstone**, where **stone remembers and fire never dies**. Before diving into serious matters, the group detoured to the town of **Timberburg** for drinks. There, they crossed paths with **Sven**, a familiar figure.

As the night wore on, **Nekis** got **spectacularly drunk**, **vomiting on himself** and **tipping over in his high chair**, much to the amusement (or horror) of the tavern patrons.

---

### **Restless Nights and Rogue Intentions**

Back at the keep, the group settled in for the night. **Ferin Blackfang**, ever loyal and protective, curled up at the end of **Aine’s bed**, standing guard as she slept. Meanwhile, **Nekis** attempted to **sneak into the Duke’s private chambers**, but was intercepted by the ever-watchful guards.

With tensions rising, **Vaelan** managed to calm the situation, **subduing Nekis with a sleep spell** and urging him to rest for the night.

---

### **Confrontation with the Duke**

In the morning, the party entered **Emberhold’s Great Hall**, greeted by the imposing presence of the **Duke and Duchess**. **Vaelan confronted his father**, pressing him about the **visions of war and corruption** he had been experiencing.

The Duke’s reply was cold and cryptic:

> _“Leave old ghosts in the past.”_

Yet, it was clear he **knew more than he was willing to say**. Suspicious of his father’s evasiveness, **Vaelan decided to investigate further**.

---

### **Secrets in the Shadows**

Sneaking into his father’s quarters, Vaelan discovered **fragments of forbidden knowledge**—**references to a “Buried Forge”** hidden deep within the mountains of Emberhold. Determined to uncover the truth, the party ventured into **the lower halls of the keep**, following the forgotten paths.

With **Thorne’s eldritch blast** clearing a **sealed passage**, they descended into the **heart of Emberhold’s past.**

---

### **The Buried Forge Awakened**

Deep beneath the keep, the party discovered **the legendary forge**—its flames long dormant but **stirring to life at Vaelan’s approach**. As he placed his sword into the forge, it **began to infuse with ancient power**.

Among the relics of the past, they noticed **suits of armor belonging to former Dukes of Emberstone**, displayed in silent vigil. But **one detail stood out—one of the suits was missing its crown**.

Before they could investigate further, **a spectral wraith appeared**, commanding the **armored suits to rise and attack**.

---

### **Battle Beneath the Mountain**

In a fierce battle against **the wraith and the animated armor**, the party fought with everything they had. The fight was brutal, but they emerged victorious—**the wraith banished** and the **ancient armors shattered**.

With the threat vanquished, **Vaelan’s sword emerged from the forge, transformed and empowered.** One side of the blade now bore **House Emberstone’s motto**:

> _“Fire and Steel”_

On the other side, a forgotten warning:

> _“That which was forged in fire must never fall into darkness.”_

---

### **What Now?**

The **Buried Forge’s awakening** raises many questions—

- Why was it sealed away?
- Who took the missing crown?
- And what dark force tried to stop Vaelan from reclaiming his legacy?

The **shadows of Emberhold’s past** have stirred… and **the flame has been reignited**.
