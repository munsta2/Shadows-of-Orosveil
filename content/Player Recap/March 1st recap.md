## Session Recap: Shadows of the Past & the Road to Emberhold**
---

### **A Whispered Warning & A Descent into the Archives**

The session began in the **laboratory of Baron Von Strik**, where he urged the group to search the **College Archives** for any records on the **mysterious orb** they had recovered. The orb itself **remained with the Baron**, securely contained in his laboratory for further study.

While discussing their next steps, **Ailyx Valehart** suddenly **felt the world distort around her**—the walls **shifted and breathed**, and a voice **whispered not from the orb, but from within her own mind**:

> _"It is time, blood of Valehart… the eclipse must rise again."_

In her **warped reflection**, she saw **her skin darken, her tusks grow, and an eerie sigil glow on her hand**—one she recognized as a **long-forgotten mark of House Valehart**. Then, just as suddenly as it began, the vision was gone. But the sigil remained, pulsing with **an eerie glow that matched the orb**.

Shaken by this **unsettling transformation**, the group decided to **rest for the night** before tackling the **forbidden knowledge hidden beneath the College**.

---

### **A Night at the Docks & An Unwelcome Proposition**

The group spent the night at a **tavern near the docks**, an old warship converted into a **bustling sailor’s retreat**. **Vaelan** performed some **magic tricks** for coin, while the party **split their accommodations**—some opting for **luxury suites**, others **choosing more modest rooms**, leading to **a mysterious itch in the morning** as a consequence.

The next day, they sought permission from **Istedel Slenderbow**, the **College President**, to access the **sealed archives**. **Drowning in paperwork** from the **attack on Baron Von Strik’s lab**, she dismissed them **as quickly as possible**, but **not before reminding Vaelan** that he was **neglecting his studies**.

As they entered the **College Archives**, they were confronted by **a mysterious figure**, who **offered to buy the orb from them**. When the party refused, the stranger **fled in dramatic fashion, shouting**:

> _"You shall rue the day!"_

---

### **Secrets of the Past & The Rise of the Archivist**

Delving into the archives, the party **discovered two ancient tomes**:

📖 **A Black Book with Gold Embellishments** – A text referencing **an ancient power of destruction**, though its **true nature remained unclear**. It spoke of **a world-shattering force that once nearly ended everything**, known as the **Verdant Tyrant**

📖 **"The Great Sealing"** – A decayed record of **noble houses involved in an ancient ritual**. The purpose of this sealing **was unclear**, as much of the text had been lost to time, but two names remained legible: **House Emberstone** and **House Valehart**.

Before they could process their discoveries, the **library shook violently**, and the **same voice from before screamed** from above:

> _"YOU SHALL RUE THE DAY!"_

With a **surge of dark energy**, the very books of the **library twisted and merged**, forming **a monstrous eldritch horror**, bound together by **cursed knowledge**. The party fought through the **storm of flying pages and grasping tendrils**, eventually **banishing the Blighted Archivist back to silence**.

---

### **The Road to Emberhold & Omens of the Eclipse**

With the **truth about the orb’s connection to noble bloodlines** now undeniable, the party set out toward **Emberhold**, the ancestral home of **Vaelan Emberstone**, seeking answers. **The orb remained behind with Baron Von Strik**, safely contained.

Yet, as they traveled, **unsettling signs** followed them:

- **A Traveler in Rags** crossed their path, muttering to himself. When approached, he turned to **Ailyx** and whispered:
    
    > _"Beware the Eclipse."_  
    > Before anyone could question him further, he **vanished** into thin air.
    
- A **Jittery merchant** offered to sell the group a **relic of great power**. However, the item was **a worthless fake**. Upon purchase the merchant vanished into thin air. That night, as the party sat by the **campfire**, the flames **twisted into the merchant’s face**, stearing into the eyes of Thorne
    
- A **strange storm** rolled in, but instead of rain, **thousands of white flower petals** floated down from the sky. A **sign of druidic magic**—but its meaning remained unknown.
    

---

### **Visions of Emberhold’s Bloody Past**

As **Vaelan drew closer to his ancestral home**, the **whispers from the orb faded**, but a **new presence began creeping into his mind**.

As the party entered the lands surrounding **Emberhold**, **Vaelan began having visions of battles long past**—faint **echoes of noble houses locked in brutal war**, fighting against **horrifically mutated creatures and other humans alike**.

Each vision was **a brutal flash of history**:

- **Knights bearing noble banners clashing with grotesquely warped humans**, their **veins pulsing with unnatural corruption**.
- **Armored warriors of House Emberstone** standing against a **tide of monstrous figures**, desperately defending the **Emberhold gates**.
- **Banners burning, bodies rotting mid-battle, and the land itself rejecting the presence of civilization.**

What **these visions mean**, or why **only Vaelan is seeing them**, remains unknown. But **one thing is clear**—the land remembers, and **it is trying to show him something**.

---

### **The Arrival at Emberhold**

**Vaelan’s visions intensifying**, and **the past clawing its way back into the present**, the party finally arrived at **Emberhold**—where **secrets, history, and long-buried truths await**.

Will **Vaelan’s family** hold the answers they seek?  
Or will they uncover something **best left forgotten?**
