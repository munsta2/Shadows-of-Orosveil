## Session Recap: Shadows, Whispers, and an Elusive Orb

## **Retrieving the Artifact**

The journey began in the darkened depths of the cave where the party successfully retrieved the **mysterious orb**. However, its influence was already taking hold. As **Nekis** clutched the artifact, the whispers in his mind grew more intense—paranoia crept into his thoughts, and the shadows around him seemed to shift and move of their own accord. His descent into madness quickened, his grip on reality beginning to slip.

Sensing the danger, **Vaelan** took the orb from him. But as soon as his fingers wrapped around its surface, a voice **spoke directly into his mind**:

> _“Free us, Vaelan… free us… Redeem your family… Right your family’s mistake.”_

The cryptic words left Vaelan unsettled, hinting at a deeper connection between his lineage and the artifact they now carried. Whatever its true nature, it was clear—the orb was no ordinary relic.

---

## **An Ambush on the Road**

As the party made their way back to the city, they were set upon by a **band of mercenaries**. Some bore the insignia of **House Varnoss**, a powerful and influential family, but others carried no such mark. Upon further investigation, it became clear that among the fallen were members of the **Nights Bane**, a group **Ferin Blackfang** was all too familiar with.

Honoring their warriors’ code, Ferin **laid the bodies of the fallen to rest**, giving them the respect they deserved, despite their allegiance. But the presence of both the **Varnoss forces** and **Nights Bane operatives** raised an unsettling question—who had truly sent them? And what did they know about the orb?

---

## **Nekis’ Madness Grows**

With the orb now out of his grasp, Nekis’ madness **intensified**. He constantly **tried to reclaim it**, his obsession growing stronger with each passing moment. When no one was looking, he **began eating the flesh of the fallen attackers**, slipping further into the abyss.

Realizing the danger he posed to himself and those around him, the party sought aid upon returning to the city. They turned to the **Church of the Everlight**, hoping their divine magic could purge whatever affliction had taken root in his mind. Yet, despite their efforts, the clergy **offered little assistance**, unwilling—or unable—to break the grip of madness.

Left with no other options, they took drastic measures—restraining Nekis inside a **sealed box**, using magic to keep him calm.

---

## **Arrival at the College & Baron Von Strik**

Seeking answers, the party made their way to **Ardenfall’s College of Arcane Studies**, hoping that Baron Von Strik, a well-regarded scholar of magical artifacts, could provide insight into the orb’s nature.

As they entered the college, however, **Nekis, even inside his box, was not silent**. He **threatened the life of Amanda, the college secretary**, sending her into a **panic**. Terrified, she fled, but not before directing the party to **Von Strik’s lab**, where the baron had been expecting them.

Within the lab, Von Strik examined the orb, **carefully placing it inside a containment unit**, wary of its dangerous properties. Seeing Nekis’ deteriorating state, he offered **potions to calm his mind**, though whether they would cure his affliction or merely dull the symptoms remained uncertain.

---

## **The Heist – A Desperate Attempt**

Just as the party began to discuss their next move, **the lab’s windows shattered**. A group of **intruders burst in**, their weapons drawn, their intent clear—they had come for the orb.

The battle was fierce. The attackers **moved with precision**, clearly professionals trained for this mission. However, the party fought back with **equal determination**, cutting them down one by one. When the dust settled and the last of the intruders had fallen, the party searched their bodies, expecting to find **clues as to who had sent them**.

Yet, no identifying marks, no sigils, no insignias adorned their armor. They were ghosts—nameless mercenaries.

The only clue they carried was a **single note**, simple yet chilling:

> _“The first group has failed. Retrieve the orb at all costs. Do not fail me. — S.”_

---

## **A Greater Threat Lurking in the Shadows**

Von Strik examined the note, his expression grim. If this was true, then there was no telling **how many more would come**.

_"This is bigger than a simple band of thieves,"_ the baron murmured, his fingers drumming against his desk.  
_"We may be dealing with something far larger… a full-fledged criminal syndicate."_

His gaze hardened as he turned back to the party.

> _"I need to study this artifact. But if these people want it badly enough to kill for it, I’ll need protection—and so will you."_

With the **city no longer safe**, and the **mystery of the orb growing ever deeper**, Baron Von Strik made the party an offer—**join him in uncovering the truth behind this artifact, and in return, he would ensure they had the resources to fight whatever enemy had set their sights on it.**

The question now was not whether to accept—but **how deep the rabbit hole would go once they did.**
